Real sailing, made playable

Shifting, gusty wind, tides, wind shadows and the racing rules. Tap the water to steer, or let the autopilot sail the course while you call the tactics.

A career worth winning

Pick your nation and race Club, National, Continental and Worlds. Top three move up, bottom three go down.

Live racing

Race real sailors boat against boat. Ranked races and regattas, levels, leaderboards, faster boats to unlock, and private races with friends.

Daily and weekly regattas

Everyone races the same wind and tide against the ghosts of the fastest sailors. Fastest time wins.

Free to play

The Club and National career and the Daily Regatta are free. Premium (£2.99 a month or £19.99 a year, 7-day free trial) unlocks the Continental and World careers, the Weekly Regatta and live racing.
